Privacy Policy

Remote Companion for Antigravity • Last updated: November 26, 2026

1. Local & Private by Design

Remote Companion for Antigravity connects your Android device directly to a companion bridge running on your own personal computer over your private network (such as Tailscale or local Wi-Fi). The developer does not operate central relay servers and does not collect, store, sell, or share your personal data, messages, screen captures, files, or audio.

2. Permissions & How They Are Used

  • Microphone & Audio: Used solely during active two-way voice sessions initiated by you to stream voice audio directly between your phone and your own PC.
  • Notifications & Foreground Service: Used to keep active voice calls running reliably with the screen off and to notify you when tasks on your PC complete.
  • Camera (QR Pairing): Used only when you choose to scan your own PC's pairing QR code to configure your private connection URL and access key locally on your device.
  • Files & Media: Used only when you explicitly attach a file to send to your PC or download a file from your PC into your phone's Downloads folder.

3. Data Storage & Third Parties

Connection settings (your personal bridge URL and access token) are stored locally on your device using Android preferences. Update checks read public release metadata from GitHub Releases without transmitting personal information.

4. Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, open an issue on the releases repository.
